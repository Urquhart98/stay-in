If you’re travelling by car, head towards Chester and follow signs for Chester City Centre. Boughton is the A51, one of the main roads leading into the city. As you approach the centre, you’ll find Waitrose Chester on Boughton — The Tudor Gathering is directly to the left of Waitrose.

If you are walking from Chester city centre, start at The Cross (the central point in town) and head down Foregate Street, passing the shops. Continue straight as Foregate Street becomes Boughton. The walk takes around 10–15 minutes, and when you reach Waitrose Chester, you’ll see The Tudor Gathering immediately to its left.

If you prefer to take the bus, many services run along Boughton from the Chester Bus Interchange. Just ask the driver for the stop at Waitrose Chester — when you get off, The Tudor Gathering is right next door, to the left of Waitrose.

Turn into Waitrose, but before heading to Waitrose Car Park, take a left, towards the Moxy Hotel Entrance.

Hot Tub House Rules

We hope you enjoy the hot tubs during your stay. To keep them safe, clean and ready for everyone, please follow the guidance below.

Keep each session to 20 minutes or less. The water is chlorinated and people can react differently to the chemicals, so we recommend taking a break after 20 minutes.

Your water is freshly prepared. We empty and refill the hot tubs after every guest check-out. Excessive splashing can affect the water level or appearance. If we are asked to attend and empty or refill the hot tub as a result, an emergency call-out charge will apply.

Please keep the hot-tub room door closed at all times. This helps contain steam and prevents the fire alarms from being triggered.

ADDITONAL GUESTS

Your contract with us is for the number of guests shown on your booking contract. Additional guests may be be charged for.

NOISE AND DISTURBANCE

If the property you're staying in has an outside area, please enjoy, but minimise use after 9:30PM to avoid disturbing our neighbours. All guests should be aware that loud music or similar noise shouldn't be heard by our neighbours. Please help us in keeping our relationships strong.

CCTV

We use CCTV on the outside areas of this property, and in some cases, we use CCTV in the communal areas of our apartment buildings to make sure everyone is safe and for complience. This CCTV is NOT actively monitored and is only used in cases of emergency and/or investigation. Interfering or tampering with our CCTV systems is a violation of our terms and guests may be asked to leave if an interference occurs.

NO SMOKING

All properties are STRICTLY NO-SMOKING. A deep clean charge of £150 - £250 is payable for any guest smoking in our properties. We employ a smoking detection system inside the apartments. If smoking outside, please correctly dispose of any rubbish and do not place in drains.

TV

Live TV IS NOT available in all of our properties. In these instances, a smart TV is available. You should use your own logins for Netflix, Prime, etc.. Please make sure you log-out of your account after use.

HAIRDRYER

A hairdryer is located with-in one of the bedrooms, usually located in a bedside table. Please return it to this location once used.

SECURITY DEPOSIT (NOT APPLICABLE FOR AIRBNB GUESTS)

Your security deposit is normally released within a few days after your check-out. This can take up to 10 working days to show on your credit or debit card, once we have released it, depending on the specific policies of your bank or card provider.

DISHWASHER

If a dishwasher is present in your property, we would be grateful if you load your used dishes and set the dishwasher to a thorough wash before you check-out. This greatly helps our cleaning team, in making sure the properties are ready for the following guests.

HEALTH AND SAFTEY

Please familiarise yourself with the location of the fire extinguisher. A first aid kit is also provided in the Kitchen Area. Please familiarise yourself with the nearest exit in case of a fire or emergency, which is normally the front or rear entrance of the property.

In case of an emergency, dial 999 and also alert our team.

IRON & IRONING BOARD

There will be an iron and ironing board, either stored in each apartment, or in the common areas. If the iron and ironing Board are shared between apartments, please return it once it has been used for the benefit of other guests.

REFUSE

Please dispose of any waste before leaving. Bin bag should be placed in the red bins at the rear of the property. If your property is in Wales, please make sure you sort the recycling, if you're in England, don't worry, it's handled by our waste collection company.

MID STAY CLEANS

If you're staying with us for a week or more, and would like a mid-stay clean, please send us a message and we will do our best to help.
